# Pacific Dataviz Challenge 2026 — sourced rules summary
Researched 26 August 2026. Primary source: official rules PDF (EN/FR bilingual, 11 pp) linked from the challenge site.

## 1. Deadline & how to submit
- Challenge runs **1 June 2026, 12:00 pm → 31 August 2026, 11:00 pm, Fiji time** (rules §4). Fiji is UTC+12 (no DST since 2021). Site timeline: "August 31, 2026 – Applications closed". HAPRI confirms "31 August 2026, 23:00 Fiji time".
- Submission is via the **online application form** (https://pacificdatavizchallenge.org/form/application-form), a multi-step Drupal webform: Applicant Type (Individual / Team / School) → Primary Applicant → Team Members → My Dataviz → Complete. Contact email: datavizchallenge@spc.int.
- Minors (<18) must attach written parental/guardian consent at registration (form provided on the site).

## 2. What must be submitted
- **Problem statement**: explanation of the problem addressed and how the dataviz responds, provided in the registration form (rules §10).
- **All data sources listed** in the submission form (homepage, Data section).
- **Static dataviz**: high-quality vector (e.g. PDF) or image (JPEG/PNG), uploaded directly via the form, **max 100 MB**.
- **Interactive dataviz**: **publicly accessible via a URL supplied by the contestant**, which **must remain accessible until at least 31 August 2029** (rules §10).
- Teams submit a single entry through one designated contact person.
- No requirement found to submit source code, repo, or reproducibility package.

## 3. Eligibility
- Main competition: individuals **or teams**, amateur or professional, **any age, any nationality, any country/territory** — subject to prize-specific criteria (§6, §12).
- Pacific schools' competition: primary/intermediate/secondary students (or equivalent) enrolled in one of 22 listed Pacific Island countries/territories on day 1 of the competition.
- Ineligible: SPC employees, jury members, other people involved in organising.
- **Entry limit: up to four (4)** distinct entries per contestant **per competition** (static-individual, interactive-individual, static-team, interactive-team) (§7). Not "one entry per person".
- Team size cap: **not specified** anywhere found.
- Previous winners: **no exclusion rule found**.
- Working languages English or French (entry, forms, jury communication); extra languages allowed if the viz is also fully in EN and/or FR (§8).

## 4. Dataset requirement (theme: climate change)
- Rule: entrants **must use one or more datasets from the published official list**; **may combine any other open data**; must cite all datasets; must comply with each dataset's licence (§9, homepage Data section). Schools may additionally use their own primary data.
- The official list is ~27 named datasets on PDH.Stat Explorer — it is **not limited to dataflow DF_CLIMATE_CHANGE(1.0)**. Many list items link to `df[id]=DF_CLIMATE_CHANGE&df[ag]=SPC&df[vs]=1.0` series (crop/livestock yield, GHG per capita, rainfall anomalies, SST anomalies, sea-level anomalies, land-cover index, etc.), but others come from different dataflows: DF_SDG_03 (TB incidence), DF_SDG_06 (drinking water), DF_SDG_11 (disaster affected persons / economic loss), DF_SDG_15 (Red List Index), DF_SDG 3.0 (renewable energy share), DF_NMDI_POP (population growth), DF_TOURISM_ARRIVALS, DF_POWER_GEN, DF_ENV_TAXES, DF_METEO_MONITOR_NET — and "Coastline" is the Pacific Data Hub *Landsat coastlines* dataset (pacificdata.org/data/dataset/landsat-coastlines), not a .Stat flow at all.
- **Synthetic / illustrative data: no rule found** either allowing or prohibiting it. Non-official additions must be "open data"; purely synthetic data would not obviously satisfy that, so treat synthetic-only augmentation as unconfirmed territory — email datavizchallenge@spc.int if needed.

## 5. Judging
- Entries assessed by a **Jury chosen by the Organiser**; decisions need not be justified; contestants waive any right of challenge (§11).
- **No judging criteria or weights are published** anywhere found (site, rules PDF, blog, FAQ-less). Only qualitative descriptors exist: the Challenge "encourages storytelling, design, innovation and technical skill" (homepage meta description). Any specific rubric (storytelling/design/accuracy weights) is UNCONFIRMED.
- Named 2026 judges (homepage): Coral Pasisi (SPC Director Climate Change & Sustainability), Salā Dr George Carter (ANU), Dr Manumatavai Tupou-Roosen (Pasifika Communities University), Eddie Trevor Osifelo (Solomon Star), Vishal Prasad (Pacific Islands Students Fighting Climate Change), Yan Holtz (dataviz engineer).

## 6. Prizes 2026 (all USD)
- Main: **Grand Pacific Prize $3,000** + optional funded participation in Visualising Climate 2026 conference (Bologna, 4 Nov 2026; at Organiser's discretion). Then per category (static AND interactive): Pacific 1st $2,000; Pacific 2nd $1,000; Pacific Youth 1st $1,000; Pacific Youth 2nd $500; Global Mention $1,500. Pacific prizes reserved for self-identified Pacific Islanders; Youth prizes also require age ≤25 at submission; Global Mentions open internationally. Total pool >$15,000.
- Schools: Grand Pacific School Prize $500; Best Melanesia / Polynesia / Micronesia School Project $250 each.
- Prizes to minors paid via consenting parent/guardian; team prizes paid to the designated contact, who handles internal distribution. Jury/Organiser may add or withhold prizes.

## 7. AI, licensing, publication, post-submission changes
- **AI**: permitted only in a supportive role (coding help, narrative, visuals). Entry must reflect the participant's own work/judgement; Organiser may disqualify entries where AI use is excessive or the piece is primarily AI-generated (§9 Originality).
- **Originality**: works must be created specifically for this Challenge, never previously published or submitted elsewhere (§9).
- **IP licence**: contestant grants SPC and the used-datasets' producers a worldwide, royalty-free, **non-exclusive** licence (reproduce/display/distribute/translate/adapt, any medium, incl. commercial, with attribution). Contestant keeps all IP and may still use/publish/commercialise (§13).
- **Publication**: "Your dataviz must be made public." (homepage rules block); interactive URLs live until ≥31 Aug 2029; winners' names/titles/links/screenshots may be disseminated; image-rights and personal-data consents apply (§15–16).
- **Post-submission updates to an entry: no rule found** (neither allowed nor forbidden). Rules themselves can be changed by the Organiser at any time; Organiser may alter/extend/cancel the Challenge (§3, §18). Unconfirmed how/whether entries can be edited after submission.

## 8. Self-hosting (e.g. GitHub Pages)
- Requirement is functional, not platform-specific: interactive entry must be **publicly accessible via the supplied URL** and remain so until at least 31 Aug 2029 (§10), plus the blanket "must be made public" rule. GitHub Pages hosting therefore appears fully acceptable **provided the repo/site stays public and up past Aug 2029** — this is inference from the quoted text; the organisers never name platforms or forbid any host (UNCONFIRMED explicitly, low risk).
